# The AI Written RFP

Prepared by the Broadcastwell team

Team review: in progress. No criterion has completed the two analyst verification process.

## The research in figures

Across fourteen categories we identify 2,003 distinct category criteria from 2,093 eligible source answers after the recorded study exclusions. The evidence contains 10,547 exact supporting answer spans, 2,417 explicit vendor association passages and 10,015 dated page comparisons across seventy mapped vendor pages.

The tables show the ten most frequently stated criteria in each category, ordered by distinct answer count and then criterion label. The full data includes every criterion and supporting record. Criteria are category specific; similarly worded criteria in different categories are not combined. Every criterion remains Team review: in progress.

## Source and scope

Answers captured under method v1.1 on 22 to 23 Sep 2026 for the Absence Index release 2026-09 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22907695).

We examine the buying requirements stated before the first vendor in each eligible category answer from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Each engine and question had three scheduled runs. We use the frozen September answers rather than a new model capture.

A criterion count is the number of distinct eligible answers that state that criterion in the eligible prefix. Multiple supporting passages in one answer count once. Its base is the eligible answer count for that category after the documented study exclusions. The reported interval is the Wilson 95 percent interval computed from that count and base. Repeated answers share prompts and conditions; these descriptive calculations are not guarantees about future answers or an independent sample of all buyers.

The repeat count shows how many of the three scheduled runs state a criterion for the named engine and question. It describes repetition in those captures. It is not a probability that a claim is correct.

## Named vendors and dated pages

A vendor association requires an eligible prefix criterion and an explicit connection to that vendor in the same complete answer. A cited publisher, generic list, anonymous product, negative comparison or unsupported implication does not establish the association. Separate passages and repeated runs are counted under the published rules.

For each category we map the five vendors with the highest mention counts in the frozen Index release, breaking equal counts by name. The page comparisons refer only to the exact dated page identified in the data. A feature or requirement not stated on that page is not evidence that the product lacks it. A partial compound statement, unsuitable product scope, missing plan detail or unread page remains unknown.

We preserve the distinction between a named product and its parent company, product suite, optional module, plan, integration, hosting variant or testimonial. Vendor claims are page statements, not tested capabilities or outcome promises. Each vendor figure appears beside the corresponding median across the five mapped vendors in its category.
